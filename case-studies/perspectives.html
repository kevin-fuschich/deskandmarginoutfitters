Case Study

Perspectives

How a simple leadership listening system helped a large organization strengthen connection, trust, and information flow during a period of significant change.

Perspectives was a monthly open forum designed to connect a small group of employees directly with the company president. Conversations were recorded, lightly produced, and distributed across the organization, allowing thousands of employees to benefit from discussions that began with just a handful of participants.

The Environment

The organization was not standing still.

Perspectives emerged during a period of extraordinary organizational transformation involving more than 15 acquisitions, a reverse merger, major technology change, organizational redesign, and a largely remote workforce.

The organization did not have an information problem. It had a connection problem. Employees wanted visibility into leadership thinking. Leadership wanted visibility into employee experiences.

The Insight

Make the conversation the program.

The idea began with a simple observation: employees consistently valued the live Q&A portion of town halls more than the presentation itself. Perspectives asked what would happen if the conversation became the entire program — and then scaled.

The Anatomy of the System

Small Conversation. Large Reach.

The innovation was not simply giving employees access to the president. The innovation was designing a system that allowed thousands of employees to benefit from conversations that began with only a few people in the room.

Why It Worked

“The authenticity already existed. Perspectives simply created a mechanism for it to travel.”

Desk & Margin Lesson

The lesson is not that every organization needs this exact program.

The lesson is that meaningful interactions can create enterprise-wide value when they are designed to travel beyond the people who originally experienced them.

Perspectives worked because it combined intimacy with scale. It created a trusted space for candid dialogue, then extended the value of that dialogue across the organization.

The most effective culture systems do not manufacture authenticity. They create conditions where authenticity can travel.

Culture strengthens when connection becomes repeatable.

Perspectives is a real example of the Desk & Margin belief that organizations do not build alignment through messaging alone. They build it through systems that help trust, context, and leadership intent move.
