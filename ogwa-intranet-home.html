The System Behind the Screen

The design is only the visible layer.

The larger work is deciding what deserves prominence, how information should move, what employees need repeatedly, which signals leadership should reinforce, and what should disappear from the homepage entirely.

Findability

Employees can get somewhere quickly. Everyday needs are treated as essential infrastructure, not buried beneath corporate content.

Prioritization

The homepage makes organizational priorities visible instead of giving every announcement equal weight.

Reinforcement

Leadership messages, operating updates, employee stories, and recurring editorial rhythms support the same organizational narrative.

The intranet becomes less of a place to publish things and more of a system for helping the organization make sense of itself.
